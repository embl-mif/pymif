zarrsmith.utils.chunks.default_chunk_size(metadata: dict[str, Any], max_size_mb: float = 100)[source]

Default chunk shape of a dataset (metadata['axes'], ['size'] and ['dtype']).

T and C chunks are 1. Z is split in 2, then Z, Y and X are all split twice more until a chunk is at most max_size_mb. Returns (chunks, chunk size in MB, number of chunks along z, y, x).
