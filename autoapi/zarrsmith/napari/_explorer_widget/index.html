zarrsmith.napari._explorer_widget

napari widget to explore the features of segmented objects, with napari-clusters-plotter.

Three things are loaded (dropped on their field, all at once anywhere on the panel, or browsed):

  • an intensity OME-Zarr (all channels and resolution levels, read lazily),

  • the labels OME-Zarr (optional: else the first group in <intensity>.ome.zarr/labels/),

  • a CSV with one row per object: a label column and any features.

A table from several images has two more columns, image_path and labels_path: the intensity and labels OME-Zarr of the image of every row, relative to the folder of the table. The intensity and labels fields are then not used: the panel lists the images of the table, the plot shows the objects of the ticked ones, and the viewer shows one image at a time (the one of the last clicked object).

The features are plotted by napari-clusters-plotter, under the panel, with everything it can do (x / y features, colour of the plot and the labels by any feature, selections to make clusters, histogram, …). The plot is linked with the viewer:

  • click a point: the viewer goes to that object (its image, its plane, centred, zoomed in) and selects its label,

  • hover a point: a thumbnail of the object (XY and XZ through its centre, the intensity with the object outlined in magenta) appears next to the mouse,

  • pick a label in napari: its point is highlighted in the plot.

Where the objects are comes from centroid columns of the CSV: centroid-0/1/2 (skimage regionprops_table), centroid_z/y/x or z/y/x, in voxels of the labels (full resolution) or in µm. Without them they are computed from a low resolution level of the labels (slow for big images, and small objects can be missed). The size of the thumbnail comes from bbox-* columns (regionprops) or a size column (area, volume, …).

zarrsmith.napari._explorer_widget._patch_plotter()[source]

Changes to napari-clusters-plotter, for the layers and the plot of this widget only.

  • It colours a labels layer by np.unique of the whole label image on every redraw (minutes for a big image) and gives the colour of row i of the features to the i-th label. The label of every row is given in the layer metadata instead (rows of an image that isn’t shown get ids that aren’t in the label image).

  • biaplotter makes the points smaller the more there are (8000 / n): with tens of thousands of objects they can’t be seen any more. A scatter plot with zarrsmith_min_size keeps that size.

  • No warning that the labels missing from the colours are transparent (wanted: they aren’t in the table), nor that a categorical colour (an image, a cluster) uses a categorical colormap.
