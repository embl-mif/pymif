zarrsmith.mosaic_manager.fields

Fields of a mosaic: groups of tiles that touch or overlap (connected components).

A mosaic can hold several separate fields, e.g. two samples or two regions of one sample imaged in the same acquisition. Fusing tiles of different fields into one image puts them on a single canvas that spans all of them, with blank space in between. Every tile therefore gets a field label when a MosaicManager is loaded, fusion uses the first field by default, and fusing tiles of more than one field is refused (MultipleFieldsError).

zarrsmith.mosaic_manager.fields.connected_components(tiles: Sequence[zarrsmith.mosaic_manager.tile.Tile], tolerance: Sequence[float] | None = None) → List[int][source]

Component index of every tile: tiles whose boxes overlap or touch are connected.

Two tiles touch when their (z, y, x) boxes (Tile.extent) overlap or are less than tolerance apart on every axis (default: one voxel). Components are numbered by their position: by the y then x then z start of their bounding box, so the numbering does not depend on the tile order.
